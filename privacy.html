2. In short

  • Aether Guard does not require an account and shows no ads. Optionally, you can sign in with Google Play Games to back up your progress to your Google account and take part in global leaderboards.
  • We do not use any analytics or tracking tools.
  • Your game progress (progress, settings, leaderboard, commander name) is stored on your device and is not sent to us.
  • The app connects to Google Play and our billing service provider RevenueCat when you open the shop or tap a purchase option, once on first launch (to look for earlier purchases) and on every launch if you have bought something (to check that your purchases are valid).

3. Downloading via Google Play

When you download the app from Google Play, Google processes data such as your Google account, device identifiers and the time of download. We have no influence on this. Google’s privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Game progress on your device

The app stores your game progress in your device’s local storage and does not send it to us. If you have enabled Android backup, Android may back up this data to your Google account; we have no access to it. You can reset your progress in the settings or delete it by uninstalling the app. The “Back up progress” feature creates a code that only you pass on.

5. Google Play Games (optional)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, the app signs you in there; otherwise you can sign in yourself in the settings. The app then stores a copy of your game progress as a “saved game” in your Google account, so it survives a reinstall or a new device, and submits your scores (total score, stars, highest endless wave) to the Google Play Games leaderboards. There they are visible to other players together with your Play Games profile name, depending on your Google Play Games settings.

Google (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) processes this data as an independent controller; we have no access to your saved game and do not store any of it on our own servers. The legal basis is providing the features you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). You can sign out of Google Play Games at any time and delete your data for this game there. Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Shop and in-app purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. You enter payment details only with Google; we never receive them.

To show prices, verify and unlock your purchases and restore earlier purchases, we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA). The app connects to RevenueCat when you open the shop or tap a purchase option, once on first launch (to restore earlier purchases after a reinstall) and on every launch if you have already bought something (to check that your purchases are still valid, e.g. after a refund). The following is processed: a randomly generated, anonymous app user ID, your IP address, the products purchased, Google Play purchase receipts, time and currency of the purchase, and technical device data (e.g. operating system version, app version, language).

The legal basis is the preparation and performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Where only prices are shown or earlier purchases are checked, we rely on our legitimate interest in providing a working shop (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). RevenueCat processes the data on our behalf under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR); transfers to the USA are based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. RevenueCat’s privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Purchase data is deleted once it is no longer needed to provide your purchases. Statutory retention periods (up to 10 years under German commercial and tax law) remain unaffected.

7. Permissions

The app only uses: internet access (for the shop and Google Play Games), Google Play billing and vibration (for game feedback, can be turned off). It does not access the camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Contact us by email or post at the addresses above.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for us is: Der Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Rheinland-Pfalz, Hintere Bleiche 34, 55116 Mainz, Germany.

9. Children

The app is not specifically directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

10. Changes

We update this policy when the app or the law changes. The version published here applies.
